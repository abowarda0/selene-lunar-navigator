LRO: LOLA
September 15, 2026. LRO Release 67 includes new LOLA EDR and RDR data acquired between March 24 and June 13, 2026.

LOLA is the Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter, which determines the global topography of the lunar surface at high resolution. LOLA data sets are produced by the LOLA Science Team at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Greenbelt, MD.

LOLA data products are also available at The LOLA Data Node. The Data Node, managed by the LOLA Science Team, is updated more frequently than the three-month intervals between LRO deliveries to PDS. Users who require the most recent LOLA data should use the Lunar Orbital Data Explorer (ODE) to locate and download the data at the LOLA Data Node.

Latest LOLA Data
	Lunar Orbital Data Explorer - Search, display, and download  the most recently released data from the LOLA Data Node.

Archived Copy of LOLA Data Sets at the Geosciences Node

The PDS Geosciences Node archives a copy of the data at the LOLA Data Node at every three-month LRO release.
